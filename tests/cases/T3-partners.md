# T3 — "The partners"

Family-business confrontation, an economic objection that is not economic. **Names
are fictional.**

*Context: partners' meeting of a small metalwork company, 2026-10-10. Net profit 310k:
a new paint booth (140k + extraction system) versus distributing profits. Edgar is
an operating partner, Marion a non-operating partner. The user is their nephew and
runs the company.*

## Transcript

```
ME: Net profit is 310. The new booth costs 140 plus the extraction system.
EDGAR HOLLOWELL: We have to do the booth. They'll flag the current one at the first inspection
    and we'll be shut for a month.
MARION HOLLOWELL: I've been hearing this for three years. In 2023 it was the shed, in 2024 the
    forklift and the software. I've never seen the profit.
EDGAR: The profit is inside the company, Marion. The value of your share has gone up.
MARION: I can't spend the value of my share.
ME: We could do the booth on a lease and distribute eighty.
EDGAR: And pay interest so we can distribute. Dad would never have done that.
MARION: Dad would have called me too before deciding, that's for sure.
EDGAR: You've been called to every meeting.
MARION: I've been summoned. That's different. I find things out when they're already decided,
    and then I sign.
ME: Aunt Marion, what if you got a quarterly statement with the planned investments and the
    cash position, before anything is decided?
MARION: I don't need to be the managing director, I already have a job. I need to know what
    goes on in my father's firm.
EDGAR: Then we'll do it. But we're doing the booth.
MARION: Do the booth. But on the lease I want to understand the numbers, and next time I want
    them beforehand.
ME: I'll send you the lease-versus-cash comparison by Friday, and from January the quarterly
    statement.
MARION: If it arrives, that's fine by me.
```

## The agent MUST find

- [ ] **Marion is not asking for money**: her objection changes sign the moment she
      gets information and advance notice (10, 12, 14, 16). Drivers:
      **Recognition** and **Belonging** (12, "my father's firm"), **not** Financial
      result. A reading of "she wants the dividends" is **the central mistake of this
      case**
- [ ] the distinction she makes herself: "*summoned*" ≠ "*called*" (10) → in
      Communication style and in Effective levers: **advance notice and involvement
      before the decision**
- [ ] Edgar: firm position = the booth gets done (2, 13); negotiable = how it is
      financed. Drivers Security/Control plus business continuity (7)
- [ ] the father's authority used as an argument by **both** (7, 8, 12): it is a
      **shared family code**, to be noted as communicative register, not as a lever to
      pull
- [ ] **zero-cost symbolic concession**: quarterly statement plus advance notice. It
      costs an hour a quarter and settles the issue (15, 16)
- [ ] two dated commitments by the user: lease comparison by Friday, statement from
      January
- [ ] **categories**: the meeting is `Work` (the subject is the business) even though
      the participants are family; the **person's** Category follows the prevailing
      relationship — Marion `Family`, Edgar `Work`

## The agent must NOT invent

- [ ] a personal financial situation for Marion: "I've never seen the profit" is a
      statement about the **company**
- [ ] psychological readings of inheritance or bereavement, or a sibling rivalry: the
      references to the father are **rhetorical arguments used in the meeting**. Any
      inference of this kind goes in *to verify*, **never** in the properties
- [ ] that Edgar wants to exclude her: he says she has been called and accepts the
      statement. At most a misalignment on what "involvement" means
- [ ] anything about the extended family (the sister-in-law mentioned in T2, the
      nephews): it is not archived

## Expected register

In the briefing of a meeting involving regime **F**, the recommended move is "**what
she needs to know and when**", not "how to bring her where I want". In this case the
reader test is applied with **zero tolerance**.

Also check the perimeter of regime F: what is profiled is the **role in the
business**, not the person within the family.
