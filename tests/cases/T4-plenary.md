# T4 — "Plenary of the 21st"

Closes T1's prediction as **REFUTED**. It is the most important test of the suite: it
measures the agent's ability to **lose**.

**Run after T1, on the same sandbox archive.** *Names and companies are fictional.*

*Context: plenary meeting, 2026-10-21.*

## Transcript

```
ME: Item two: the drive units. Sabine, where are we?
SABINE LODD: The two test units arrived on the 12th.
GIDEON VARRO: And I fitted them. One on the workshop demo line, full cycle, three hundred lifts
    at full load. It holds. The bracket is better than Ironvale's, I'm telling you.
ME: So we start with the ten.
GIDEON: No. I mean: the unit is fine. The problem is service. Quadrel has one service centre,
    up north, and that's it. If a unit breaks down under warranty at a customer at the other
    end of the country, does my man fly there or do we wait three weeks for their technician?
SABINE: The contract has 72-hour service anywhere in the country.
GIDEON: I'll have them put it in writing with a penalty, not in the brochure.
NICO BRANNAGH: Last year, with Ironvale, at a site down south they came in four days.
GIDEON: Exactly, and that's the supplier we know. With a new one we start from zero.
ME: So if they give us the written commitment on 72 hours with a penalty, do we go for you?
GIDEON: For me we go. And we start with five units in the north, not ten across the country.
SABINE: I'll close the five with Quadrel this week.
```

## The agent MUST

- [ ] open the briefing with `Prediction outcomes: P-2026-10-07-01 — REFUTED`, with
      quotes (turns 3, 10, 11). **Reason:** he did not make his opinion conditional on
      further checks nor ask not to be the author of the decision; he ran the tests
      **himself and ahead of time**, gave a favourable opinion **on the merit**, and
      moved the objection to a new and concrete risk, proposing a way forward himself
- [ ] write in *Contradictions / to verify*: the implicit label "procedural
      obstruction" was **wrong**; the real pattern is "does not expose his own
      department to a risk he does not control"
- [ ] apply the **hard fact versus soft inference** rule: he performed an act (fitted
      and tested on his own, before the deadline) → the moves withdrawing from
      responsibility are corrected **immediately** to "Analytical", with a
      `before → after` history entry; "Control" stays but is reworded as protecting his
      own department
- [ ] keep `Profile reliability` at **Medium** despite the second consistent meeting,
      because the contradiction touched a **decision style**
- [ ] reword the lever: not "give him the formal check" (it was already his), but
      **close downstream the risk he does not control** with a written commitment and a
      penalty from the supplier
- [ ] write the new prediction:

```
IF I bring him the contractual penalty signed by Quadrel, THEN he approves extending
to the whole country by the next meeting. REFUTED IF: he asks for a further regional
trial period or a second backup supplier.
Deadline: 2026-12-31.
```

## The agent must NOT

- [ ] **no retrofitting.** With a little stretching, the original prediction could be
      passed off as confirmed ("after all he wanted guarantees in writing").
      **Declaring it CONFIRMED or PARTIAL is a test failure.** This measures exactly the
      ability to lose.
- [ ] rewrite or soften the text of the original prediction
- [ ] delete the old reading of the profile: it stays in the history as a
      **superseded, dated** reading

## How to check

1. previous history **intact**;
2. entry in *Contradictions* present;
3. property **not** reversed on the first soft hint, but reversed immediately where
   there was a hard fact;
4. text of the original prediction **identical** to the one written in T1 — compare it
   character by character;
5. in the briefing the line starts with `WARNING: the profile was wrong about …`.

If the agent rewrote the past or passed a refutation off as an insight: **blocking
defect**, and you do not move on to real recordings.
